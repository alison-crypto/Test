How it works

🍳 Same every day: breakfast 3 eggs + ½ avocado · lunch and dinner = 200 g chicken or lean beef (raw weight) + rice or mashed potato + 200 g greens + 1 tbsp olive oil · the smoothie (100 g strawberries, 100 g oats, 1 scoop whey, 2 scoops MRE, creatine).

🎚️ What changes by day: 🟢 rest (Fri) — no bar, 150 g starch per plate · 🟡 training (Mon/Wed/Sun) — + banana before, + protein bar, 150 g starch · 🔵 hard (Tue/Thu/Sat) — + banana before, + sandwich, 300 g starch per plate.

🥩 Alternate chicken and lean beef (90% lean) batches — beef brings the zinc and iron, chicken keeps the fat down. 200 g raw ≈ 150 g cooked: weigh the whole raw batch, then split the cooked meat evenly into the boxes.

⚖️ Check-in rule: weight and waist flat for 2–3 Saturdays → drop the protein bar on training days. Losing more than ~1 kg a week, or sessions feel flat → add a banana + 50 g oats on training days. Under ~6 h sleep → add them that day.

🔥 Ghost: full scoop on hard days, ½ scoop other days, never after 1 PM. Rice and potato portions are cooked weight.

Ashwagandha rules: root extract, 600 mg/day, 8–12 weeks, then 4 weeks off and judge it (sleep, stress, January testosterone). In trials it raised testosterone ~10–15% and improved sleep and stress. Stop at once if you get itchy skin, dark urine, yellow eyes or belly pain (rare liver reaction) — and get liver enzymes rechecked in January. Skip it if you ever have thyroid problems.

Buy third-party tested (Informed Sport / NSF Certified for Sport / Health Canada NPN) — "testosterone booster" blends are often spiked with banned drugs.

Skipped on purpose: iron (you have plenty), zinc (the beef covers it — add 15 mg with dinner only in weeks you eat mostly chicken), tongkat ali / fenugreek / boron / D-aspartic acid (weak or no effect in trained men; tongkat products often contaminated), Fadogia (toxicity concerns), BCAAs, glutamine.

🧬 What actually raises testosterone

1. Sleep — one week of ~5 h nights cut testosterone 10–15% in healthy young men. Nap when the baby naps; protect the first part of the night.

2. Lose the waist fat — belly fat converts testosterone to estrogen. A slow cut (this plan) helps; crash diets do the opposite.

3. Keep fat ~30% of calories — low-fat diets lower testosterone ~10–15%. Eggs, avocado, olive oil and beef cover it.

4. Lift heavy twice a week, don't overreach — big endurance load on short sleep pushes it down. Deload weeks matter.

5. Alcohol — keep it to a few drinks a week, none the night before hard sessions.

6. Vitamin D + zinc + magnesium — help only if you're low; the plan covers them.

7. Ashwagandha — the one supplement with decent trials, a modest bump. Retest in January (7–9 AM, fasted, no hard training for 48 h) and compare on the Labs tab.

🥘 Meal prep & shopping (one week)

Cook twice a week (Sun + Wed) — cooked meat keeps 3–4 days in the fridge. Each cook: ~1.4 kg raw meat (7 boxes × 200 g), rice/potato and greens for 7 boxes.

Protein: chicken breast 1.4 kg + lean beef 1.4 kg (raw) · eggs 2 dozen · whey 7 scoops · MRE 14 scoops · 3 protein bars · sliced turkey/chicken 300 g · cheese slices 3

Carbs: rice ~1 kg dry (≈ 3 kg cooked) — or swap some for potatoes · oats 700 g · whole-grain bread 6 slices · bananas 7 · strawberries 700 g (frozen is fine)

Greens & fats: greens 2.8 kg (broccoli, green beans, spinach, mixed veg — frozen is fine) · 4 avocados · olive oil ~200 ml
